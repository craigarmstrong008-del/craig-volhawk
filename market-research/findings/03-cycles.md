## 1. Key findings

1. The four-year halving cycle rests on N=3 completed cycles (2012-14, 2016-18, 2020-22; 2024-26 is the 4th, still open) plus the 2012-13 start. Critics make the sample-size, multiple-testing, non-falsifiability and survivorship arguments (https://protos.com/basic-statistical-flaws-of-bitcoins-four-year-price-cycle ; https://eco3min.fr/en/crypto-seasonality-cycles-signal-illusion/). Defenders cite tops 525/546/534 days after halvings, identified retrospectively, and concede that it is three points (https://quantpedia.com/bitcoin-runs-on-a-clock-why-every-price-indicator-dies-and-the-halving-clock-doesnt/). Honest read: timing fits are descriptive, not statistically established.
2. The current cycle has so far behaved like the "cycle" camp predicted: all-time high about $126,080 on 2025-10-06 (https://cryptoticker.io/en/bitcoin-price-62000-crash-fed-hike-60000-level-november-15-2026/ via search summary), roughly 13 months later price is about $62-68k, ~50% below ATH. This is one more data point for a 4-point sample, not proof. Amplitude has shrunk each cycle (https://blockworks.co/news/bitcoin-halving-cycle-returns).
3. Galaxy Research (June 2026, secondary coverage) says the four-year cycle persists but the halving's influence is fading, only 4 of 13 bottom indicators had triggered, base-case bottom $40-46k, washout $30-37k, shallow case $51-54k (https://cointelegraph.com/markets/bitcoins-calm-top-challenges-old-bottom-targets-what-traders-need-to-know ; https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/galaxy-research-bitcoin-s-current-cycle-bottom-not-formed-expected-at-40-000-46-000). Previous bottoms came 777-912 days post-halving; the 2024-04 halving + that range = roughly June-Oct 2026 which has already passed or is passing, so the "time-based" bottom window is arguably now. (Arithmetic ours; halving date 2024-04-20 not re-sourced.)
4. Competing views on bottom: CryptoQuant realized-price floor ~$55k with a 4-6 month base, MVRV not yet in historical bottom territory (https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-02-13-cryptoquant-bitcoin-ultimate-bear-market-bottom-55000-389919); a July 2026 low near $57.8k exists and breaking it would imply a 2018-style final leg (https://beincrypto.com/bitcoin-halving-clock-bear-market/ via search summary); 13-year-pattern view says bottom not yet in (https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/bitcoin-bear-market-may-take-longer-to-bottom-analyst-cites-13-year-pattern); Galaxy's earlier piece headlined "bear market is over" (https://decrypt.co/381786/bitcoin-bear-market-is-over-galaxy-research-says) - note the same shop flipped, which itself illustrates narrative instability.
5. On-chain indicators (MVRV, NUPL, realized price): all validation we found is in-sample on ~3-4 cycles and vendor-sourced (BIT claims MVRV Z<0 marked every bottom "with zero false signals": https://www.bit.com/research/bitcoin-bear-market-over-on-chain-indicators). No out-of-sample backtest found. Different outlets quote incompatible MVRV/NUPL values (standard vs Z-score), so even the current reading is ambiguous.
6. Liquidity: level correlation of BTC with M2 is high but spurious-prone; monthly-change correlation is ~0.04 (https://www.bit.com/knowledge-hub/global-m2-decoupling); lagged correlation peaks ~0.5 at 1-2 months for Jan 2022-Jan 2026 (same source); CF Benchmarks reports the relationship broke down in Q4 2025 (https://www.cfbenchmarks.com/blog/the-m2-bitcoin-relationship-what-the-data-actually-shows). "Global M2" is not an official series; results depend on construction.
7. Stablecoin supply is contracting right now: DefiLlama ~$294.8B on 2026-11-09, -1.54% w/w (https://stablecoininsider.org/stablecoin-supply-snapshot-2026-11-09/); The Block reports a $14.2B contraction, largest since the 2022 bear (https://theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2026-11-11-stablecoin-supply-drops-14-2-billion-largest-contraction-since-2022-bear-market-437907); peak ~$309.7B in May 2026 (https://cryptobriefing.com/stablecoin-market-cap-drops-21b-since-may/). Whether stablecoin supply LEADS price or follows it is untested here.
8. Seasonality: Oct/Nov strongest by median, Dec weak, but means are dominated by outliers (Nov 2013 +450%) and samples are ~13 observations per month (https://finance.yahoo.com/news/november-october-analyst-says-bitcoin-213609745.html ; Coinglass figures reproduced at https://news.bitcoin.com/price-predictions/uptober-hype-shaky-bitcoin-most-predictable-months-keep-failing/). Day-of-week: mixed academic evidence, no persistence (Baur et al. via https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:finlet:v:31:y:2019:i:c:p:78-92 ; https://quantpedia.com/are-there-any-simple-calendar-effects-in-bitcoin-market/). Treat as weak.
9. Structural intraday/weekend change post-ETF: weekend share of BTC volume fell from 24% (2018) to ~16-17% (Kaiko via https://investinglive.com/cryptocurrency/bitcoin-isn-t-a-weekend-hedge-it-s-just-the-only-market-still-open/); US hours share of spot volume ~47% vs 38% pre-ETF (https://dailycoin.com/bitcoins-weekend-gap-etfs-shift-liquidity-to-u-s-hours/). This is the most plausible "real" calendar structure (liquidity-driven), but return-direction claims by session conflict (Parafi vs CryptoSlate/Kaiko, see table).
10. Prediction markets: volume is event-driven and lumpy. Record Polymarket international ~$10.8B in June 2026 (World Cup) then -26% to $7.9B in July (https://www.theblock.co/post/410382 ; https://blog.portals.fi/prediction-markets-volume-2026/); Kalshi dominates sports (~87% share per one source), Polymarket leads politics/crypto in March 2026 (https://pm.wiki/uk/learn/prediction-market-volume-records-march-2026). Midterm volume spiked into 2026-11-03; post-election drop-off not found.

## 4. Testable hypotheses (pass/fail rules, data required)

Common rules for all: pre-register before looking; split data into in-sample and a held-out final block; count every variant tried and apply Bonferroni or a deflated-Sharpe haircut; include fees (perp taker ~5bp/side assumed, verify per venue) and funding; require the edge to hold in BOTH BTC and ETH and in the post-Jan-2024 ETF era alone.

H1. Time-since-ATH drawdown base rate. Question: conditional on being 380-440 days after a cycle ATH, what is the forward 90-day BTC return distribution? Data: daily BTC close 2011-2026 (3 completed analogues + now). PASS only if all 3 analogues' forward-90d median has the same sign AND we state it as N=3 and refuse to size above exploratory. Practically this is a FAIL for tradability by design (N too small); its use is to bound narrative, not to trade.

H2. MVRV/realized-price floor as out-of-sample entry filter. Rule: buy when spot < realized price (or MVRV<1) and BTC at least 40% below ATH; exit at 365 days. Data: daily BTC price, realized cap (Glassnode/CryptoQuant/Coin Metrics community CSV). Also apply to ETH and to each prior ETH/BTC cycle using ETH's own realized price. PASS: positive 12-month return in every cycle including held-out ETH 2018/2022 episodes, AND worst drawdown after entry < 35%. FAIL if any episode has post-entry drawdown >35% (2018 and 2022 both likely do; test it). Expected honest outcome: signal works for 12m, fails for timing.

H3. Stablecoin supply impulse. Rule: 4-week change in total stablecoin cap (DefiLlama) < -1.5% => next 4-week BTC/ETH return lower than unconditional. Data: DefiLlama daily stablecoin cap since 2020, daily BTC/ETH. PASS: t-stat on non-overlapping 4-week windows > 2.5 (after accounting for autocorrelation, use block bootstrap), same sign in 2022 and 2025-26 sub-periods, and reverse-causality check (does BTC lead stablecoins by 1-2 weeks? if lead-lag shows BTC leads, FAIL). Sample warning: ~300 non-overlapping 4-week windows at best.

H4. Macro tightening regime filter. Rule: define risk-off when (10y real yield 3-month change > +50bp) OR (DXY 3-month change > +3%) OR (HY OAS up >100bp in 60d); test whether BTC 4-week forward return is lower and vol higher in risk-off. Data: FRED DFII10, DTWEXBGS or DXY, BAMLH0A0HYM2, daily BTC. PASS: lower median forward return with p<0.05 after block bootstrap AND the effect exists in post-2023 only data; otherwise treat as a vol (not direction) filter. Also test the BIT/CF finding that M2-change correlation is ~0 on our own series.

H5. Fed-meeting event window. Rule: compare BTC return from 24h before to 24h after FOMC statement, for hawkish vs dovish surprise (use fed funds futures implied change vs actual). Data: FOMC dates, CME/FedWatch odds history, BTC 1-min bars. PASS: sign of reaction matches surprise in >= 65% of at least 25 meetings, with mean |move| > 2x fees and slippage. December 8-9, 2026 is the next live test; write the rule down before then.

H6. Weekend/low-liquidity mean reversion. Rule: weekend (Fri 21:00 UTC to Sun 21:00 UTC) moves > 2 sigma of trailing 30-day weekend vol are followed by partial reversal by Monday 14:00 UTC. Data: 1-hour perp prices, volumes, funding (Binance/Bybit/Hyperliquid) 2021-2026. PASS: positive net-of-cost expectancy in each year 2022-2026, N>=40 signals per year. FAIL if edge exists only pre-2024 (ETF decay).

H7. US-session drift. Rule: long BTC from 13:30 UTC to 20:00 UTC (NYSE hours) vs rest of day, ETF era only. Data: hourly bars 2024-01 to 2026-11. PASS: session spread significant at p<0.01 on daily-block bootstrap and positive in at least 4 of 5 half-year blocks. Note Kaiko and Parafi conflict, so a null result is likely.

H8. Month-of-year. Rule: long Oct-Nov, flat Dec-Sep. Data: monthly BTC returns 2013-2026. PASS: monthly hit rate difference significant after Bonferroni over 12 months (alpha 0.05/12), and still positive when excluding Nov 2013 and 2017. Expected: FAIL.

H9. BTC dominance / alt-season regime. Rule: alts (ETH, SOL, top-20 ex-stables) outperform BTC over next 90 days only after BTC.D falls >3 pts in 30d AND BTC makes a new 90d high. Data: CoinGecko/CMC dominance history, top-N prices (survivorship-bias-free universe including delisted). PASS: top-quintile excess return over BTC positive in each of 2017, 2021, 2024 episodes AND positive after a delisting-inclusive universe. Current state: no alt-season conditions met (no new BTC high), so this is a filter to STAY OUT of alt-beta.

H10. Prediction-market event calendar. Rule: for Polymarket sports/election markets, mispricing (longshot overpricing; final-days convergence) is larger in the 14 days around peak-volume events (World Cup, Super Bowl, election). Data: Polymarket/Gamma API trade history, resolved markets by category since 2022 (sandbox cannot reach it; run from a normal machine). PASS: calibration slope deviation (price vs realized frequency) in event windows > non-event windows by a margin whose 95% bootstrap CI excludes zero, with >=300 resolved markets per bucket and net of fees/spread.

## 6. Ways a trader loses here (traps)

1. Treating N=3 as a law. Four-year timing fits have more free parameters (anchor date, tops vs bottoms, day counts) than data points. When price deviates the cycle gets "re-drawn" (non-falsifiable). Size cycle-based bets as small, exploratory.
2. Calling the bottom early because of time. Time-based windows are passing but price-based and on-chain signals (4 of 13) are not confirming; analysts who called "bear is over" were wrong in sequence. A late-cycle bear typically has multiple false bottoms and sharp relief rallies that stop out shorts and longs both.
3. Averages instead of medians in seasonality. Nov +41% mean is one 2013 observation. With 13 observations per month and 12 months, a "best month" will always exist by chance.
4. Spurious level correlations (BTC vs M2, vs Fed balance sheet, vs anything trending). Monthly-change correlation near zero. Liquidity narratives are rationalised after the fact, and the sign of the relationship flips across regimes.
5. Regime break: 2025-26 is a case where the liquidity relationship "broke" and ETF flows plus rate expectations dominate. A backtest through 2020-2022 describes a different market (pre-ETF, different participants).
6. Hiking cycle with strong growth is an unseen mix for crypto (the 2022 analogue had weak growth). Leveraged longs into a Dec 9 FOMC with hike pricing in flux risk a gap move; liquidity thins in extreme fear (F&G 13) and cascades are the main way perps accounts die.
7. Weekend and Asia-hours folklore. Weekend volume share is falling and ETF flows moved liquidity to US hours; old weekend/Asia patterns decay. Weekend lower volume means worse slippage and stop hunts, not an edge.
8. Max-pain and "expiry magnet" stories: the 5-of-8 hit-rate is what you would see if price simply stays near where it already is.
9. Alt-season hope. Dominance 58-61%, index below 75 through mid-2026, ETH/BTC at multi-month lows. Alt perps carry funding, delisting and survivorship risk; backtests on current top-100 are survivor-biased.
10. Prediction markets: comparing volumes across Kalshi (notional) and Polymarket (USD at price) is apples-to-oranges; event-driven volume spikes bring informed flow and fee/spread friction. Post-event droughts cut liquidity and widen spreads, so edges measured in peak windows may not transfer. Midterm markets had concentrated "handful of bettors" pricing per the ACDC analysis (https://acdatacollective.org/?p=1662), so apparent edge may be single-whale noise.
11. Narrative-fitting on vendor research (Galaxy, CryptoQuant, BIT, CF Benchmarks all sell products). Their "zero false signals" claims are in-sample.
12. Stacking five weak filters and calling it confirmation: five correlated cycle signals are one signal.
